Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Codexa: Learn Code ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Leoryx, handles user information. We are committed to transparency and respecting your digital privacy.

Codexa is fundamentally designed as an offline-first educational application. We do not require account registration, and your lesson progress, bookmarks, and quiz scores are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development and offer free coding education, Codexa integrates third-party advertising services:

  • Google AdMob (Google Play Services): Used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / IDFA), IP address, app performance metrics, and general location data to serve relevant ads.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy regarding advertising technologies here: Google Privacy & Terms for Ads.

3. Device Permissions & Local Storage

Codexa does not request dangerous device permissions such as access to your camera, contacts, microphone, or precise GPS location.

All user progress, completed modules, and streak data are maintained in local on-device storage (SharedPreferences). You may delete all stored app data at any time through your device's application settings or by uninstalling the application.

4. User Privacy Choices & Ad Personalization

You have control over how ads are served on your device:

  • Android Devices: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising by navigating to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
  • EEA / UK Users: When applicable, consent for personalized ads and cookie usage is managed in accordance with GDPR via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

5. Children's Privacy

Codexa does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe personal information has inadvertently been collected, please contact us immediately so we can promptly address the issue.
